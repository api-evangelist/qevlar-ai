# Qevlar AI

> Qevlar AI is an autonomous SOC platform that investigates every security alert like your top analyst would — without playbooks, without hallucinations, and without adding headcount. Trusted by Fortune Global 500 enterprises and leading MSSPs across Europe and the US.

Qevlar AI was founded in 2023 by Ahmed Achchak and Hamza Sayah, two machine learning engineers. The company is headquartered in Paris, France, and is SOC 2 Type 2 certified. The SaaS platform is hosted in the EU (Belgium) on GCP.

## What Qevlar AI does

Qevlar AI automates alert triage, enrichment, and investigation across the full security stack. As soon as an alert is triggered from SIEM/EDR tools, Qevlar autonomously pulls, enriches, and analyzes data from internal and external sources — then delivers a conclusive verdict (malicious or benign), a comprehensive investigation report, and recommended remediation actions.

Key metrics: investigations completed in under 3 minutes, up to 80% of tickets closed automatically, 24/7 continuous coverage with no backlog.

Unlike LLM-based agents, Qevlar's core engine is a proprietary graph-based AI orchestrator that delivers deterministic, evidence-based reasoning — ensuring consistent and reproducible results across every investigation.

## Who it's for

- **Enterprise SOC teams** struggling with alert overload, analyst burnout, and missed threats
- **MSSPs and MDRs** needing to scale investigations across multiple tenants without adding headcount
